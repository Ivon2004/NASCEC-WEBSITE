PRESERVING OUR ROOTS, PROTECTING OUR FUTURE.

Harnessing the power of indigenous storytelling to champion cultural heritage and environmental conservation in Vihiga County

Welcome to Native Storytelling for Culture and Environmental Conservation (NASCEC) CBO, a community-powered initiative born in the heart of Vihiga County. In NASCEC, we believe that the indigenous wisdom carried in our elders' stories holds the key to a sustainable green future. Our mission is simple yet profound: to revive our rich cultural ecological consciousness and restore our natural environment by bridging generations through the timeless art of native storytelling. We are not just conserving; we are re-activating the deep, indigenous knowledge that did sustain our community for centuries.

WHAT WE DO

Cultural Revival

Documenting and celebrating Luhya heritage through oral histories, songs, and proverbs.

Environmental Action

Translating traditional ecological knowledge into tangible conservation projects.

Intergenerational Bridge

Creating spaces where elders and youth share, learn, and grow together.

Community Resilience

Building a more sustainable and culturally vibrant Vihiga, one story and one tree at a time
